      Frequently asked questions

      How does FoldTube work?

      FoldTube gets YouTube tabs out of your way without losing them. Click the FoldTube icon, or press , and every open YouTube video is saved here and its tab is closed. Press to save just the tab you're on, and to come back to this list. When you're ready to watch, click a video to open it again.

      Why do videos disappear after I open them?

      FoldTube is a list of things to watch, so opening a video takes it off the list. That way the list only holds what you haven't watched yet. Opened several at once by mistake? Click Undo in the message at the bottom of the page. Want to keep one for later? Save its tab again.

      Which tabs get saved automatically?

      None, unless you turn on Auto-collapse in Settings. With it on, FoldTube saves the YouTube videos in any window you close, and all of them when you quit the browser. Those appear here the next time the browser starts. Closing a single tab never saves it, and if your browser reopens your tabs, they aren't saved twice.

      Why wasn't a YouTube tab saved?

      FoldTube saves videos, Shorts and live streams. Other YouTube pages, like the home page, search results, channels and playlists, are left open.

      Why is a video's channel or duration missing?

      Videos saved from a sleeping or closed tab arrive without their page details. FoldTube looks up the channel the next time you're online. The duration needs a free YouTube API key, and so do the details of videos that can't be shown on other sites. You can add a key in Settings → Advanced.

      Where are my saved videos stored (and can I back them up)?

      Only in this browser, on this device. They don't sync, and uninstalling FoldTube deletes them. To keep a copy, open Settings → Advanced and choose Export to JSON. Import from JSON brings them back, here or on another computer.

      Is my watch history private?

      Yes. FoldTube has no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking, and your list never leaves your browser. To fill in channel names, it asks YouTube about a video by its ID, without your cookies or sign-in. If you add an API key, it also asks Google's YouTube API, using your key.

      Why does nothing happen when I fold in Firefox?

      Firefox asks you before an extension can read a website. Open about:addons, choose FoldTube, open Permissions, and turn on access to youtube.com.
