      Frequently asked questions

      How does FoldTube work?

      FoldTube clears YouTube tabs out of your way so you can watch them later. Click the FoldTube icon or press to save and close every YouTube tab open in your browser. does the same for just the tab you're on, and brings you back here. Click any video when you're ready to watch it.

      Why do videos disappear after I open them?

      FoldTube is your watch-later list, so opening a video takes it off. Opened several by mistake? Click Undo at the bottom of the page. To keep one for later, save its tab again.

      Which tabs get saved automatically?

      Only when Auto-collapse is on in Settings. Then FoldTube saves the YouTube tabs in any window you close, and all of them when you quit the browser. Closing a single tab never saves it.

      Why wasn't a YouTube tab saved?

      FoldTube saves videos, Shorts and live streams. Other YouTube pages, like the home page, search, channels and playlists, stay open.

      Why is a video's channel or duration missing?

      FoldTube can't read a tab that was asleep or already closed, so it looks up the channel once you're online. Durations need a free YouTube API key, which you can add in Settings → Advanced.

      Where are my saved videos stored (and can I back them up)?

      Only in this browser, on this device. They don't sync, and uninstalling FoldTube deletes them. To keep a copy, choose Export to JSON in Settings → Advanced; importing it brings everything back, on any computer.

      Is my watch history private?

      Yes. FoldTube has no servers, accounts or tracking, and your list stays in your browser. To find a channel name, it asks YouTube about the video by its ID, without your sign-in. If you add an API key, it asks Google's YouTube API as well.

      Why does nothing happen when I fold in Firefox?

      Firefox asks before an extension can read websites. In about:addons, open FoldTube → Permissions and allow youtube.com.
